Gini impurity of a node = 1 − Σ(pᵢ²), where pᵢ is the proportion of class i in that node. A split's weighted Gini = (n_left/n_total)×Gini_left + (n_right/n_total)×Gini_right. Gini decrease = parent Gini − weighted Gini; CART picks the split that maximizes this decrease.
